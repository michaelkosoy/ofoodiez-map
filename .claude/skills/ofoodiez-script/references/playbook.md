## 2. Ofir's own performance patterns (his data, 6-month window to 2026-07-24)

- **Keyword-CTA reels crush the rest:** median **~84K plays / 4.78 comments per 1k views** vs **~37K /
  0.95** without a keyword CTA → ~**2.3× reach, ~5× comments**. He used it on only ~26% of reels. This
  is his single biggest lever — push toward using a CTA on ~100% of reels.
- **Reels ≫ carousels:** reels median ~548 likes vs carousels ~73 (~7×). Prefer reels; film carousel
  ideas as voiceover reels.
- **Best hooks (by like-rate):** **price/value reveal** ("שילמתי 80 ₪…", "מה קנינו ב-30 ₪?") — his
  top resonance; **numbered list + FOMO** ("3 מסעדות חדשות…"); **pattern-interrupt skit** ("סליחה,
  איפה את עובדת?"). Flat "יש מקום חדש ש…" opens underperform.
- **Duration:** his **>40s reference-dense reels outperform** his short ones (median ~53K vs ~24K) —
  do NOT force 8–15s; keep density, just nail the first 2s.
- **Reach ≠ resonance:** his 433K-play French-bistro reel had the worst engagement (1.5 likes/1k).
  Copy the high-engagement model (Japan value-reveals at 22–26 likes/1k), not raw-reach flukes.

## 4. The winning mechanics (what they do EXACTLY) — clone these

1. **Curiosity-gap first line** — Cleo: *"What would happen if Earth was actually flat?"* (6.4M, ER 58)
   · *"Can you guess this animal?"* (6.3M). A wild question or a visual promise, then "it's weirder than
   you think." Highest-ER format. Visual-first: every sentence = an on-screen visual.
2. **Provocative / contrarian claim + a concrete rule** — Codie: *"You're falling behind for one reason —
   you won't stop talking"* → the 80/20 rule (4.2M). Bold claim → a rule/number → memorable.
3. **"Comment [word] for the link"** — Rowan's *"Comment 'Director'"* → **4,317 comments** on one reel
   (2.1M). Exactly Ofir's ManyChat mechanic, proven at scale. Give a real reference in the DM.
4. **"Let me unpack this"** explainer — zauey: *"Are we in an AI bubble? Let me actually unpack this"*
   (931K) — one confusing hot topic, who-does-what step by step with graphics. Ownable recurring name.
5. **Weekly funding/news roundup** — zauey: *"the hottest startup rounds this week"* (832K) — 3 items,
   each "who raised $X to solve what → how → traction". Recurring, ownable.
6. **Famous-founder ambush interview** — tradedvc: *"Hey, are you Bill Gates's daughter?"* (1.9M) /
   *"Mr. Trump!"* (2.5M). The famous name is the hook → one-line pitch → a quotable tip.
7. **AI made human/tangible** — Rowan: *"a broccoli farmer in Japan automated his farm with ChatGPT,
   never studied engineering"* (1.1M, ER 70). Relatable underdog makes AI concrete.
8. **Sticky rule-of-three** — "come for the coffee · stay for the people · leave with a brand bond."
9. **Debate-question close** — Codie: end on a two-sided question → comments.

Cross-cutting: hook in 2s (spoken **+** on-screen text) · one idea · recognizable recurring format ·
show the payoff first · concrete names/numbers · selfie-cam > studio · pattern-interrupt early.

## 5. 2026 Instagram algorithm rules (why the above works)

- **Shares / DM-sends are the #1 ranking signal; likes are among the weakest.** "You can't bait a
  share." → build "send/tag" and "comment-for-DM" into every reel.
- **3-second retention gates non-follower reach** (>~70% watch-past-3s → wider distribution). Pattern-
  interrupt hooks retain best. Put the interrupt by ~0:03.
- **On-screen text in the first frame is non-optional** — most reels are watched muted.
- **Saves come from reference content** — checklists, maps, guides. Ofir's "recommendations map" DM is
  a save magnet.
- **Selfie-cam / raw > studio** — Instagram deprioritizes over-produced content.
- **ManyChat comment→DM via the official API is safe** (<0.5%/yr suspension) but keep DM copy varied &
  human; cap ~200 auto-DMs/hr; Meta tightened enforcement on generic automation since late 2025.

## 7. Pulling creator data (optional, heavier — only for fresh first-party numbers)

Ofir authorized use of his logged-in Instagram session. Approach that worked (be gentle — throttle +
backoff to avoid an action-block):

1. **Session:** `instaloader` in a venv; `--load-cookies firefox` once to create a session file, then
   `load_session_from_file(...)`. (`browser_cookie3` needed.)
2. **Enumerate + metrics:** instaloader's post-pagination is broken on current IG — instead hit the
   web-app endpoint with the authenticated session: `GET https://www.instagram.com/api/v1/feed/user/{uid}/?count=33&max_id=...`
   with header `x-ig-app-id: 936619743392459`. Each item has `code, play_count, view_count, like_count,
   comment_count, video_duration, caption, video_versions[]`. Paginate via `next_max_id`; **pinned posts
   appear first out of order** — don't early-stop on one old post; stop when a whole page is older than
   the window, then filter by date.
3. **Video/transcribe:** the `video_versions[]` URLs expire fast and 403 if fetched with cookies —
   download the **smallest** one **with a plain browser User-Agent, no cookies, immediately** after
   fetching (fresh). Extract audio with macOS `afconvert` (no ffmpeg on this Mac) → 16k mono WAV →
   local `mlx-whisper` (`mlx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo`, cached). The CDN throttles after
   ~15 rapid downloads → add per-download backoff + a delay between reels; wrap each account in
   try/except so one throttle doesn't kill the run.
4. Direct reading of the Firefox cookie SQLite is blocked by the sandbox classifier — use instaloader's
   `--load-cookies` path instead.
